Printing tips for students and parents

  • For a quick dorm refresh, print at 8×10, 11×14, or 12×16 and use lightweight frames.
  • For a gallery wall, keep spacing consistent and test the layout on the floor before hanging.
  • Use matte or satin paper to reduce glare from overhead dorm lighting.
  • Check the Etsy listing description for included file sizes before sending files to a printer.
