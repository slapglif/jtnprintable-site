Lake House Guest Bedroom Wall Art Ideas

A lake house guest bedroom works best when the wall art feels calm, coordinated, and easy for visitors to understand at a glance. This printable six-piece coastal collection uses blue hydrangeas, sailboats, shells, lighthouse details, and cottage neutrals for a finished guest-room gallery wall.

View the Lake House Printable Set on Etsy

Digital download only; no physical prints, frames, or mats are included.

Best places to hang the set

Use the full six-print set above a queen bed, over a dresser, beside a reading chair, or along a short cottage hallway outside the guest room. The repeated blue-and-white palette keeps the room restful while still adding a clear lake-house theme.

Guest-room layout options

  • 3-by-2 grid: the simplest option above a headboard or dresser.
  • Pair plus shelf: print two larger pieces for the main wall and rotate the remaining four on shelves.
  • Hallway extension: place three prints in the bedroom and three in the adjoining hall for a connected suite feel.

Printing note for rentals and cottages

For vacation rentals, use matte paper behind acrylic rather than bare glass when possible. It reduces glare from windows and bedside lamps and is easier to maintain in busy guest spaces.
