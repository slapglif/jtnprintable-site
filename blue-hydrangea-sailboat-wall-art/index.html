How to style a blue-and-white coastal entryway gallery wall

Use this six-print set as a calm focal point in a beach-house, lake-house, or cottage entryway. Start with the hydrangea and sailboat designs near eye level, then place the lighthouse, shorebirds, cottage, and shell artwork around them. The repeated blues and soft neutrals keep the arrangement coordinated without making a narrow hall feel crowded.

Choose a layout before printing

  • Narrow console: arrange all six prints in a balanced 3-by-2 grid.
  • Long hallway: use a single row of six matching frames.
  • Small landing: hang four prints in a 2-by-2 group and place the remaining two in nearby rooms.

Make paper templates in your intended frame sizes and tape them to the wall first. Keep spacing consistent—about 2 to 3 inches works well for compact gallery walls—and center the complete arrangement over the furniture rather than centering one individual print.

Paper and frame suggestions

Heavyweight matte paper helps the pale blue and linen tones read softly and reduces glare in bright coastal rooms. White, light oak, or natural wood frames reinforce an airy cottage look; slim navy frames add contrast. This collection includes 72 JPG files: six designs in 12 print-ready sizes, so choose the included file that exactly matches each frame rather than stretching or cropping it.
